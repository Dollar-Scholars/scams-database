What happens next

  1. We save your report. It is added to the database with the status Pending.
  2. It joins the community data. ScamDB generates reports on the latest scams to help other individuals and organizations.
  3. Your details stay private. Your name, email and phone number are never shown on public pages.

If you have been scammed

  • Stop replying to the scammer and do not send any more money.
  • If you paid or shared card details, call your bank or card provider straight away.
  • Change any passwords you shared and turn on two-step verification.
  • Keep screenshots, messages and receipts as evidence.
  • This form does not alert the police or your bank. Report it to them as well.
